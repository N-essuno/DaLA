# Dataset Card for C5 Filtered

<!-- START-SHORT DESCRIPTION -->
Creative Commons licensed Dutch web pages from [C5](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BramVanroy/CommonCrawl-CreativeCommons)
<!-- END-SHORT DESCRIPTION -->

C5, the Common Crawl Creative Commons Corpus, is a filtered derivative of Common
Crawl that keeps only pages carrying an explicit Creative Commons licence. GPT-NL further filtered it on permissive license. The collection is mostly Dutch open web:
news and opinion sites, blogs, community and interest-group pages, and
educational and religious material.

## Dataset Description


<!-- START-DESC-STATS -->
- **Number of samples**: 56.93K
- **Number of tokens (Llama 3)**: 47.49M
- **Average document length in tokens (min, max)**: 834.33 (5, 82.35K)
<!-- END-DESC-STATS -->



## Dataset Structure
An example from the dataset looks as follows.


<!-- START-SAMPLE -->
```py
{
  "id": "c5_filtered_0",
  "text": "Wie niet van kolonialisme wil spreken moet ook over racisme zwijgen - de twee hangen zo nauw samen d[...]",
  "source": "c5_filtered",
  "added": "2026-09-07",
  "created": "2019-07-01, 2025-02-28",
  "token_count": 508
}
```

### Data Fields

An entry in the dataset consists of the following fields:

- `id` (`str`): A unique identifier for each document.
- `text` (`str`): The content of the document.
- `source` (`str`): The source of the document (see [Source Data](#source-data)).
- `added` (`str`): The date when the document was added to this collection.
- `created` (`str`): The date range when the document was originally created.
- `token_count` (`int`): The number of tokens in the sample computed using the Llama 3 tokenizer.
<!-- END-SAMPLE -->

## Additional Information

### Source Data

The pages come from [C5](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BramVanroy/CommonCrawl-CreativeCommons),
built from Common Crawl in collaboration with GPT-NL, and were released after
further filtering as the `common-crawl` collection of the
[GPT-NL Public Corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GPT-NL/GPT-NL_Public_Corpus).

### License Information

Only pages that declare a Creative Commons licence in their markup are kept, and
GPT-NL manually reviewed the licence declarations of the largest domains before
release. GPT-NL records the licence of every document in this collection as
CC-BY-4.0.

### Limitations

Licences are read from the markup of a page, so a page is included on the strength
of what its publisher declared; a wrong or over-broad declaration on the source
site carries through into the data. Grouping by domain means a handful of large
sites contribute a large share of the documents, and the opinions expressed on
them are not representative of Dutch web writing at large. Boilerplate such as
navigation, comment forms and cookie notices survives the extraction in places.

### Citation Information

This dataset is derived from the [GPT-NL Public Corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GPT-NL/GPT-NL_Public_Corpus). We recommend that you reference the corpus if you use this dataset. The annotations were generated by [tvosch/GPT-NL-propella-annotations](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tvosch/GPT-NL-propella-annotations).
