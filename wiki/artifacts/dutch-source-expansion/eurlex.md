# Dataset Card for MultiEURLEX

<!-- START-SHORT DESCRIPTION -->
Dutch versions of European Union legislation from [MultiEURLEX](https://huggingface.co/datasets/coastalcph/multi_eurlex)
<!-- END-SHORT DESCRIPTION -->

MultiEURLEX is a multilingual collection of European Union legal acts drawn from
EUR-Lex, the official database of EU law, with each act available in the
languages of the Union. This subset holds the Dutch versions. The documents are
regulations, directives and decisions of the Council and the Commission, written
in the fixed structure of EU legislation: a title and act number, the recitals
that state the legal basis and reasoning, and the numbered articles that carry
the operative provisions, often followed by technical annexes. The acts date from
1958 to 2016, with the bulk from the 1980s onward.

## Dataset Structure
An example from the dataset looks as follows.


<!-- START-SAMPLE -->
```py
{
  "id": "eurlex_0",
  "text": "UITVOERINGSBESLUIT VAN DE COMMISSIE\nvan 21 augustus 2012\novereenkomstig Richtlijn 95/46/EG van het E[...]",
  "source": "eurlex",
  "added": "2026-09-07",
  "created": "1958-01-01, 2016-12-31",
  "token_count": 4495
}
```

### Data Fields

An entry in the dataset consists of the following fields:

- `id` (`str`): A unique identifier for each document.
- `text` (`str`): The content of the document.
- `source` (`str`): The source of the document (see [Source Data](#source-data)).
- `added` (`str`): The date when the document was added to this collection.
- `created` (`str`): The date range when the document was originally created.
- `token_count` (`int`): The number of tokens in the sample computed using the Llama 3 tokenizer.
<!-- END-SAMPLE -->

## Additional Information

### Source Data

The acts come from the [MultiEURLEX](https://huggingface.co/datasets/coastalcph/multi_eurlex)
dataset, which is derived from [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/), and were
released as the `multi-eurlex` collection of the
[GPT-NL Public Corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GPT-NL/GPT-NL_Public_Corpus).

### License Information

EUR-Lex documents may be reused under the Commission's reuse policy, which allows
reproduction and adaptation with acknowledgement of the source, and MultiEURLEX
is released under CC-BY-SA. GPT-NL records the licence of every document in this
collection as CC-BY-4.0.

### Limitations

EU legislation is drafted in one language and translated into the others, so the
Dutch here is translated legal prose and carries the sentence structure of the
drafting language rather than of Dutch legal writing. Every act follows the same
structure, which repeats across documents. Because the same act is
present in MultiEURLEX in many languages, near-duplicate content exists in the
source across languages, and the coverage stops in 2016.

This dataset is derived from the [GPT-NL Public Corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GPT-NL/GPT-NL_Public_Corpus). We recommend that you reference the corpus if you use this dataset.
